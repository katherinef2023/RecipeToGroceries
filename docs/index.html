How to use

Welcome to Recipes to Groceries, your personal meal planner that creates a smart grocery list based on meals you select. Simply click "Add to List" on any recipe that sounds good and it will automatically add the ingredients to your grocery list, which you can access by pressing the "≡" button then "Grocery List". If you want to create/share your own recipes, please make an account by clicking the profile image in the top right. Then, you are free to add and publish recipes. You can also use the annonymous login (email: "dummyemail@gmail.com" and password: "password") to do the same.

Add Recipe

Put the recipe title in the first box, ingredients in the 2nd, and instructions in the 3rd box, then add the recipe! For the ingredients: new lines (press "enter") and semicolons (;) work as seperators. Please put a numerical value (0.33, not 1/3) as the first part of each ingredient.
